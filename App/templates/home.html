Our Mission

Our team believes in the empowerment of individuals to obtain agency over their personal breast health. We strive to make our service as accessible to a vast majority of the global population, in an effort to reduce the incidence and impact of breast cancer. Additionally, we hope to encourage open dialogue amongst individuals to allow for a healthy discussion regarding breast health.

Why Early Diagnosis?

Early diagnosis in breast cancer is absolutely essential. Cancer cells may remain dormant for the majority of their lifespan and metastasize without the individual even knowing. According to the American Cancer Society (ACS), breast cancer is the most common cancer among American women.

"Patients are experiencing better outcomes as a result of early diagnosis, state-of-the-art treatment options, and less extensive surgery." — Catherine Tuite, MD, Fox Chase Cancer Center

How It Works

Our AI analyzes digitized images of a fine needle aspirate (FNA) of a breast mass. By inputting simple metrics like radius, texture, and perimeter, our algorithm can predict diagnosis likelihood.

We recommend consulting your doctor to obtain these metrics from a biopsy. Once entered, we will email you a thorough analysis of the next steps.
